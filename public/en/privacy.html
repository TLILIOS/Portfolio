Data collected by this site

This site is a static portfolio: it contains no data collection form, no advertising tracker and no audience analytics tool. The site itself sets no cookies.

Language preference (localStorage)

Your FR/EN language choice is stored locally in your browser (prim.lang key, localStorage). This data is strictly functional, stays on your device and is never sent to any server. You can delete it at any time through your browser settings.

Scheduling (Calendly)

The "Schedule a call" section uses the third-party service Calendly LLC. The calendar is only loaded after you explicitly click the display button: no Calendly resource (nor its associated cookies — Calendly, Cloudflare, Stripe) is loaded before this voluntary action.

If you book a slot, the data you enter (name, email address, chosen slot, optional message) is processed by Calendly as a data processor, exclusively for the purpose of scheduling the appointment. See Calendly's privacy notice.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare Workers). Cloudflare may collect technical logs (visitor IP addresses) for security and service-maintenance purposes. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Emails

If you contact me by email, the data you provide (address, content) is used solely to answer your enquiry and kept for the duration of the business exchange, at most 3 years after the last contact.
